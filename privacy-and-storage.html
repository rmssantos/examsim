Privacy & Data Storage

This page describes the standalone local edition. The separate paid online service at examplar.app uses accounts and personal licences and has its own storage policy. New online purchases require an internet connection and do not include offline packs.

Your data stays local. Exam content, answers, progress, imports, images, editor changes, and preferences are stored in your browser unless you explicitly export and share them.

Your data stays local. The app stores study data in browser storage: IndexedDB for larger exam data and images, with localStorage for small settings and legacy compatibility.

Stored in your browser

Imported exams, images, recent attempts, progress, editor changes, theme preference, and local exam visibility settings.

Not sent to the server

Question text, selected answers, imported files, filenames, personal progress, study history, names, emails, and account identifiers.

No analytics or telemetry

This final local edition contains no telemetry client, tracking hooks, ingestion configuration, campaign storage, or ad-click forwarding on any hostname. Local progress statistics, attempt review, and missed-question study actions remain on your device. There is no analytics setting to opt out of.

Earlier downloads and historical commits retain their original behavior. Update the app files and reload to replace old cached assets. Legacy analytics preferences left by an old version are unused.

Network requests and external links

The browser requests app files and pack resources from your local server. Pack images are loaded from local or same-origin files, or from IndexedDB. Following a study resource, GitHub link, or complete-exam link contacts that destination under its own privacy policy. Normal browser referrer rules apply; this edition does not append campaign identifiers, ad-click identifiers, emails, licence keys, answers, or local storage to links.

The separately maintained online service has its own privacy policy. A self-hosted server may keep access logs controlled by its operator.

Imports and editor changes

Dragging an exam file into the app or saving changes in the editor updates only that browser profile. Export the corrected JSON for backups or your own fork. This frozen repository remains available to download and does not accept new changes.

With python server.py, the editor can explicitly upload validated image files to the local pack folder. It does not upload question dumps. The server binds to loopback by default.

Offline behavior

After the app shell is cached, the simulator works offline for the pages and assets already installed by the service worker. This privacy page is cached so the footer link opens as a readable app page instead of downloading a raw file.

Self-hosting and shared exams

A server can pre-install exams in user-content/exams/. Those static exam files are visible to every user of that deployment, but each user's progress and imported private exams still stay in their own browser storage.

Storage locations

  • IndexedDB: imported exam content, images, image metadata, progress, and recent attempt review summaries.
  • localStorage: theme, exam activation settings, and legacy compatibility mirrors.
  • sessionStorage: short-lived local navigation and study selections.
  • Cache Storage: app shell files needed for offline use.

Backups

Browser storage is scoped to the site origin and browser profile. Clearing site data can remove imports and progress. Use the export actions when you need a backup or want to move data to another browser.
