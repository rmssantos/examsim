# What passing AB-731 proved to me

## Draft copy

When I passed AB-731, I had prepared entirely with Examplar.

That was the first proof I wanted from the project — but it was not proof that
the same workflow works for everyone.

It showed me three narrower things:

- the local practice and review loop was usable for a complete study cycle;
- the product could support the study goal it was built for;
- maintaining the bank matters after the pass, not only before it.

Microsoft published an AB-731 objective version dated 22 July 2026. I reviewed
the 120-question Examplar bank against it and updated the affected Foundry Tools,
Microsoft 365 Copilot, and licensing terminology.

The official audience is business decision-makers leading AI transformation.
Microsoft explicitly says candidates are not expected to write code, so the
practice scenarios should test business value, adoption, governance, and tool
selection rather than programming trivia.
