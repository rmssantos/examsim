# A practical AI Engineer learning sequence

## Draft copy

An AI certification roadmap should be a learning sequence, not a fake
prerequisite chain.

The AI Engineer path in Examplar is:

AI-901 → AI-103

Microsoft positions AI-901 for someone at the beginning of AI solution
development. Its objectives cover AI concepts and practical implementation with
Microsoft Foundry.

AI-103 moves into the Azure AI engineer role: planning and managing solutions,
building generative applications and agents, and implementing vision, text,
speech, and information-extraction workloads.

That makes the sequence useful:

- AI-901 establishes the concepts and Foundry workflow;
- AI-103 tests broader implementation and operational decisions.

This is an Examplar learning path, not a Microsoft prerequisite. If you already
build Azure AI solutions with Python, starting at AI-103 may be the better use
of your time.
