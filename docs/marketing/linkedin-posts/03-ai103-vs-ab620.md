# AI-103 and AB-620 are different agent paths

## Draft copy

AI-103 and AB-620 both talk about AI agents.

They are not interchangeable certifications.

AI-103 is aimed at an Azure AI engineer who builds, manages, and deploys AI
applications and agents with Microsoft Foundry. Its scope also includes computer
vision, text analysis, and information extraction, and Microsoft expects Python
development experience.

AB-620 is aimed at a professional developer or advanced builder working with
enterprise agents in Copilot Studio. Its guide calls out Power Fx, Dataverse,
Power Platform, Microsoft 365 Copilot, APIs, MCP, and multi-agent integration.

A practical way to choose:

- choose AI-103 when your role centres on Azure AI applications and Foundry;
- choose AB-620 when your role centres on extending and integrating agents in
  Copilot Studio.
