# A small organic experiment

## Draft copy

No paid ads. No marketing spend. The next Examplar experiment is deliberately
small: can useful, source-led certification posts produce actual study starts,
not just reactions?

The plan:

- two source-led posts per week for eight weeks;
- one tracked deep link per post;
- no paid acquisition;
- measure landing → configured session → first answer → completion;
- use repeated study intent, not reactions alone, to choose the next pack.

This is the part of building in public that I find useful: state the hypothesis,
define the measurement first, and make the next decision from evidence.
