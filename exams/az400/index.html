Frequently asked questions

Is the AZ-400 practice exam free?

The 25-question AZ-400 preview is free and works without an account. The complete exam is a separate paid online service requiring an account and internet connection. No offline download. See the hosted service for current pricing.

Are these real AZ-400 exam questions?

No. These are original, syllabus-aligned questions written to match the official objectives for Designing and Implementing Microsoft DevOps Solutions. They are not copied from the live exam.

Can I use this as a timed AZ-400 practice test?

Yes. Exam mode runs timed practice based on the public AZ-400 objectives, and study mode adds instant feedback with spaced repetition.

Does my data stay private?

In this local simulator, questions, answers, imported content, and progress remain in your browser. The public site uses limited opt-out product telemetry; local and private self-hosted use does not initialize analytics. The separate paid online service has its own account and storage policy.

How many AZ-400 questions are included?

The local preview includes 25 questions. The online AZ-400 Complete includes 280 questions.
